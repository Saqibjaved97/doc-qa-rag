# Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

All accounts must use the Microsoft Authenticator app as a second factor. SMS codes are no longer accepted since January 2026.

## Setting up the Authenticator
Install Microsoft Authenticator on your company or private phone, open aka.ms/mfasetup on your laptop and scan the QR code. Approve the test notification to finish.

## New phone
Before you reset or trade in your old phone, open aka.ms/mfasetup and add the new phone first. If the old phone is already gone, the service desk can clear your MFA methods after checking your identity.

## Hardware keys
Employees who cannot use a smartphone can request a FIDO2 security key through the service desk at no cost.
