# Home office equipment

Employees who work from home at least two days a week can order home office equipment: a monitor, a docking station, a headset, a keyboard and a mouse.

## Ordering
Use the Self-Service portal under "Home office set". The items are shipped to your home address within 10 working days.

## Desk and chair
IT does not supply furniture. Ask HR about the yearly home office allowance of 300 euros for a desk or chair.

## Internet at home
Nordwind does not pay for private internet connections. If your connection is too slow for video calls, a mobile hotspot can be lent for up to 4 weeks.
