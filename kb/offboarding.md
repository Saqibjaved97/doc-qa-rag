# When an employee leaves

The manager submits the "Leaver" form at least 5 working days before the last working day.

## Accounts
The account is disabled at 18:00 on the last working day. The mailbox is kept for 90 days and can be shared with the manager on request.

## Returning equipment
Laptops, phones, badges and security keys must be returned to the IT office on the last day. Personal files must be removed before the last day; IT wipes all devices.

## Handover
Files in OneDrive are transferred to the manager automatically after the account is disabled.
