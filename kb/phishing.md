# Reporting phishing and suspicious emails

If an email looks suspicious, do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook; it sends the email to the security team and removes it from your inbox.

## If you already clicked
Change your password immediately at reset.nordwind.example and call the service desk on extension 4000. Speed matters: the security team can block your sessions before damage is done.

## Typical signs
Unexpected invoices, urgent requests to buy gift cards, sender addresses that look almost like ours, and login pages that are not on nordwind.example.

## Phone scams
IT will never ask for your password or MFA code by phone. Hang up and report the call.
