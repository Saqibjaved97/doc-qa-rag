# Printing

Every floor has a multifunction printer named after the floor, for example PRN-F2 on the 2nd floor. Print jobs are held until you badge in at the device (follow-me printing).

## Adding a printer
Printers are installed automatically on company laptops. If a printer is missing, open Software Center and run "Printer setup".

## Paper jam or toner
For paper jams, follow the animation on the printer screen. Toner and paper are in the supply cabinet next to each printer. If the printer still shows an error, report it with the printer name in the ticket.

## Printing from home
Printing to office printers from home is not possible. Save the document and print it at the office.
