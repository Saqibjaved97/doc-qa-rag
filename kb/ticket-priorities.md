# Ticket priorities and response times

The service desk sorts tickets into three priorities.

## High
The whole team or a production system is affected, for example the warehouse scanners or payments are down. Response within 30 minutes, also on weekends.

## Medium
One person cannot work normally, for example a broken laptop or no VPN access. Response within 4 working hours.

## Low
Questions, requests and minor issues, such as new software or a missing printer. Response within 2 working days.

## Escalation
If a ticket is not answered within its response time, call the service desk on extension 4000 and give the ticket number.
