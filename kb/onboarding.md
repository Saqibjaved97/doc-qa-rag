# Accounts for new employees

The hiring manager requests accounts for a new employee at least 10 working days before the start date, using the "New employee" form in the Self-Service portal.

## What the new employee gets
A company account, an email address, a laptop with the standard software, MFA set-up instructions and access to the groups chosen in the form.

## First day
New employees collect their laptop at the IT office between 8:30 and 10:00 on their first day and set up their password and MFA together with a technician.

## Interns and working students
Interns and working students get the same setup, but their accounts expire automatically on the contract end date.
