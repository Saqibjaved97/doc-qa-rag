# Ordering a new laptop or other hardware

Standard laptops are replaced every four years. You can check the age of your device in the Self-Service portal under "My devices".

## Requesting hardware
Open the Self-Service portal, choose "Order hardware" and select the item. Requests above 500 euros need approval from your manager. Delivery to the office takes about 5 working days.

## Broken laptop
If your laptop is broken, bring it to the IT office. You get a loaner laptop the same day while yours is repaired. Accidental damage is covered, but please describe what happened in the ticket.

## Monitors and accessories
A second monitor, a headset, a docking station, a mouse and a keyboard are standard equipment and do not need approval.
