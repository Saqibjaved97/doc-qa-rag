# Resetting your password

Company passwords expire every 180 days. You get a reminder email 14 days before.

## Self-service reset
Go to reset.nordwind.example, enter your username and confirm with the Authenticator app. The new password must have at least 14 characters and must not reuse any of your last 5 passwords.

## Locked account
After 10 wrong attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes. If you cannot wait, call the service desk on extension 4000; they will verify your identity with your employee number before unlocking it.

## Forgot password and no phone
If you have lost access to the Authenticator app as well, the service desk must reset both. Bring your ID card to the IT office on the 2nd floor.
