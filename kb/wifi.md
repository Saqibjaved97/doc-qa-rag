# Wi-Fi in the offices

There are two networks: NW-Corp for company laptops and NW-Guest for visitors and private devices.

## Company laptops
Laptops connect to NW-Corp automatically with a certificate; you never need a password. If NW-Corp is missing, restart the laptop while connected by cable so it can renew its certificate.

## Guests
Visitors get a NW-Guest voucher from reception, valid for 24 hours. Guest Wi-Fi only allows internet access, not internal systems.

## Slow connection
Wi-Fi is slow in the large meeting rooms when many people stream video. Use the cable ports on the tables for important presentations.
