# Connecting to the company VPN

Nordwind uses GlobalConnect as its VPN. You need the VPN to reach internal systems such as the file server, the HR portal and the warehouse dashboard from outside the office.

## Installing the client
Open the Software Center on your laptop, search for "GlobalConnect" and click Install. On private devices the VPN is not allowed; use the web portal at portal.nordwind.example instead.

## Connecting
Start GlobalConnect, enter the gateway vpn.nordwind.example and sign in with your normal company account. You will be asked for a second factor in the Authenticator app.

## Common problems
If the VPN disconnects every few minutes, switch from Wi-Fi to a cable or turn off power saving for the network adapter. Error 812 means your account is not in the VPN group; ask your manager to request VPN access through the service desk.
