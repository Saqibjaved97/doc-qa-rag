# Working abroad and travelling

Company phones include roaming in the EU and Switzerland. For other countries, request a travel data package in the Self-Service portal one week before you leave.

## Working from another country
Working remotely from abroad for more than 10 days needs approval from HR because of tax and data protection rules. The VPN may be blocked in some countries; check the list on the intranet before travelling.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report lost or stolen laptops and phones immediately to the service desk, day or night, on +49 931 555 4000. IT can lock and wipe the device remotely.
