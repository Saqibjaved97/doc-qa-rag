# Video meetings

Nordwind uses Microsoft Teams for meetings and calls. Meeting rooms have Teams Room systems that start the meeting with one touch.

## Meeting room problems
If the screen in a meeting room stays black, check that the touch panel is on and press "Join" again. For audio echo, make sure only one laptop in the room is connected to the meeting audio.

## External participants
Guests can join from the browser without installing Teams. Webinars with more than 300 participants must be booked with IT two weeks in advance.

## Recording
Recordings are stored in OneDrive for 120 days. Tell all participants before you start recording.
